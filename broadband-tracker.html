Tracker · Public broadband money

Idaho’s broadband money, tracked.

Idaho has $583.2 million in federal BEAD funding to bring good internet to places that lack it. This page follows who holds that money, who gave it back, and how far the projects have actually gotten. It is rebuilt from state records every week.

Where the projects are

Signed is not built.

Each project has to clear these stages in order. The state reports progress through signing. It has not published counts for the stages that put fiber in the ground, so those stay empty here until it does. source source

  2. Agreement signed

    45 projects

    As of the Oct. 1 board packet, which also says 43 in one passage.

  3. Environmental clearance

    Not reported

    Required before any digging. The packet gives no count.

  4. Construction

    Not reported

    Cannot begin until environmental review is complete.

  5. Service live

    None reported

    The state’s packet reports no BEAD-funded connection in service.

Where the money is

More than a third of the awards came back.

Four providers did not trim their plans. Each returned every Idaho award it had won, which we confirmed by matching the state’s withdrawal rows against its February award table, provider by provider.

Who withdrew

4 providers, 25,386 locations.

The board packet lists the withdrawals but gives no reasons. A location is a home, business or other building the state counts as needing service; it is not a count of people.

What we don’t know yet

Open questions.

  1. Which counties and towns were in the withdrawn project areas, and who now covers them. The state’s location spreadsheets hold this; county-level tracking is our next addition.
  2. How many of the 45 signed projects have cleared environmental review, and when the first construction starts.
  3. Why the four providers withdrew. The state’s packet does not say.
  4. What the required low-cost internet plan will cost subscribers in each area.
  5. Which locations, if any, are still unclaimed after the re-bid. The state calls its current estimates preliminary.

Notes on the numbers

The packet prints the withdrawal total as $164,352,678.01 and 25,393 locations. Its own rows add to $164.32M ($164,322,362.55) and 25,386 locations, the same figures as the four providers’ February awards. We use the row totals.

The packet says 45 projects have signed agreements in two places and 43 in another. The February overview’s table counts 727 project areas while a note on the same page says 588.

Method

How this is built.

Every figure comes from Idaho Office of Broadband and Idaho Commerce documents listed here. We transcribed the provider tables, then checked each one by adding every row and comparing it with the document’s own totals. The tracker was compiled with AI and reviewed by the editor before publication. No interviews, site visits or provider contacts were part of this version.
