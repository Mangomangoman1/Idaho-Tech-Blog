## Synthesis and uncertainty

Days served, operating span and frequency are separate timetable attributes.
Seven-day service would change the weekly pattern available along this proposed
corridor, but all-day does not establish 24-hour service or suitability for an
individual shift/course. A return trip and any connection also have to exist.
These are our practical analysis, not a tested itinerary, employer schedule or
claim to have interviewed a rider. Not every listed destination is necessarily
reachable on another route at the same hours. No new fare inferred from existing
Route 42 or regional fare tables.

We use only mutually supported weekday/Saturday/frequency facts, avoiding endpoint
versus stop-specific times and the page/brochure span difference. No travel-time,
