## Synthesis and scope

Running code, getting permitted use and obtaining support are distinct product
questions. Publicly funded research software can appear under different licenses;
free prerequisites do not establish the license of the application. Web delivery
can change who handles installation/hosting without defining all use/distribution
rights. These are operational implications, not a legal interpretation for a user.
Readers should inspect the actual offering and terms when evaluating a tool.
