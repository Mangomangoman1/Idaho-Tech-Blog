## Editorial synthesis and boundaries

Three tests answer different questions: non-nuclear heat-transfer engineering,
near-zero-power reactor physics, then later useful heat/electricity integration.
The April 2025 report supplies evidence for this distinction rather than merely
rewriting an announcement. Source descriptions/forecasts remain attributed.

Public schedule statements differ: October 6 release anticipates December criticality;
DOE and INL project FAQs checked today list 2027 dry criticality/2028 full operation.
Article names both and chooses no firm startup date. No allegation about motives,
compliance or engineering competence. No current completed criticality claimed.

We have not accessed the approved final documented safety analysis, independently
assessed reactor safety, verified operating output, final end-user agreements,
commercial economics or a utility procurement. No claims about lower power bills,
Idaho grid capacity, project profitability, delivered household supply or a new
commercial license. No new account, external request, outreach or interview.
