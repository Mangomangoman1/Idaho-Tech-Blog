## Original synthesis

Evaluate the source library as a governed product: who owns/approves the document,
which version is authoritative, how changes reach a tool and who checks the answer.
Illustrative record questions are ours, not a court-issued template or implemented
system. No actual incorrect answer, obsolete court manual or deficient county is
asserted. A citation/source link helps someone check an answer but is not proof of
correctness; human validation, access/privacy and permitted use still matter.

The reader payoff is an accountable way to read a future public AI announcement:
look for the use case, authority for sources, responsible reviewer and evidence of
testing. The current report is not proof of reduced processing time, cost savings,
improved outcomes, an approved public legal chatbot or automated judicial decisions.
The analysis concerns internal administrative knowledge work, not case instructions.
