## Editorial synthesis and limits

Fresh hook: October's multi-jurisdiction call-handling agenda, read alongside
approved GIS decisions and the posted funding design. Reader payoff: a shared
technology purchase does not describe every local operating responsibility.
Useful public questions concern recurring service cost, who maintains address/
boundary data, and which budget supports ordinary dispatch operations. These are
our analysis, not allegations of noncompliance, waste, understaffing or unsafe
service. No county vulnerability, location accuracy, response time, availability,
outage, network layout, protocol detail, call-routing configuration or security
weakness is disclosed or inferred. No test call or system access performed.

FY2026 application deadline predates this article; do not advertise it as open.
No total current cost, per-resident bill, county fee rate, signed October contract,
October vote or complete statewide installation status verified. Do not combine
historical grants into a made-up current budget. Maps/data and staff are ongoing
work; no performance guarantee follows from purchase, grant or approval.
