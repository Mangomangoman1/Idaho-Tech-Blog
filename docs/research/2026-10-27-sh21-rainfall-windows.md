## Synthesis and editorial scope

Useful finding: the published products answer different questions; an accumulated
total cannot supply an unobserved shorter peak. A monitoring proposal would need
to specify location, interval, interpretation and responsibility for updates. This
is our analysis, not an official trigger or an instruction to buy a system.
Readers get the local report, its methodological context and the current official
travel-information route. No claim a particular basin/property is safe or unsafe.
