## Synthesis and uncertainty

Verified distinction: IC3's intake/referral and Finance's regulatory complaint
process describe different responses. A bank's urgent transfer response is another
function. Our analysis: a future shared intake should explain receipt, referral,
ownership, cross-references, permissions and how it counts reports and outcomes.
These are editorial questions, not announced state specifications or findings that
any institution mishandled a case. No particular victim, bank or offender named.

The paper form's question about another agency provides a concrete public-document
hook for continuity across reports. It does not prove that databases already join
or that one submission reaches every necessary institution. No actual report was
traced, and no statewide response-time, staffing, procurement cost, vendor or
