## Synthesis and limits

Reader payoff: adopting a reporting portal and replacing a payroll application are
different decisions. Agency FAQ offers upload; a vendor logo alone does not prove
format compatibility. Data transfer also differs from an authorized certification
and project review. These are our operational analysis, not measured contractor
outcomes or legal advice about an individual agreement. Link agency instructions
for project-specific coordination, rather than asserting every Idaho road job or
every consultant is subject to identical Davis-Bacon wage obligations.

No allegation of noncompliance, system defect, security weakness or misconduct.
No security comparison repeats the agency’s marketing claim as an independent
finding. No agency procurement contract, licensing cost, migration completion,
logged-in interface, payroll file, worker record, payment timing or actual savings
was inspected. No personal payroll data collected or reproduced. Original diagram
will show the two publicly described input routes, not an unobserved interface or
actual sequence of certification screens.
